# GPT-6 (gpt-6-astra via codex, medium, web search): what SF2 players watch vs the U-arm questions (2026-10-01)

Brief: docs/research/perception_research_brief.md. Independent, blind to the Claude report.

**The five questions capture useful basics, but miss the timing and direction that make those basics actionable.** Experienced players watch changes: entering range, leaving the ground, an attack missing, recovery beginning, contact becoming hit or block. Their reactions depend on practiced responses and expectations—not just recognizing a pose.

Scope matters: **SNES Super Street Fighter II has no super meter.** Much classic community material concerns Super Turbo or HD Remix; their perceptual principles transfer, but exact timings, throw rules, and move properties require verification in the target ROM. [Version clarification](https://www.reddit.com/r/StreetFighter/comments/kmt6j1)

**What players say they watch**

| Player account | Screen moment: general perception | Knowledge or history needed beyond the moment |
|---|---|---|
| **Spacing and whiffs.** Maj teaches stepping briefly into poke range, backing out, then exploiting the response. Footsies consists of practiced situations that become fluid through repetition. [Footsies, chapter 1](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=691) | Gap, closing/separating movement, limb extension, attack missing, withdrawal. | Effective ranges; which response reaches before recovery ends; whether previous approaches provoked attacks. |
| **Startup recognition can be fooled.** Maj describes fast attacks impersonating slower ones, inducing a mistaken punish attempt. Similar early animations can be difficult to distinguish. [Chapter 2](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=759) | Something started; approximate attack direction and phase; whether it contacted. | Exact animation identity, duration, and vulnerability. “Movement detected” does not establish a punishable commitment. |
| **Attention is selective.** Thelo describes preparing a small set of reactions, with heightened attention around landing, wake-up, and recovery ending. He also describes preparing directional inputs before the visual trigger, leaving only the final button press. [Reaction-based defense](https://forums.sirlingames.com/t/thelos-quick-guide-to-reaction-based-defense/1043) | Jump onset and direction; recognizable transitions; when someone becomes mobile again. | Which response to prepare, input readiness, expected timing, and likely opponent behavior. |
| **Projectiles are separate threats.** Maj stresses that projectiles remain independent of their owner; travel distance changes the interaction and the owner’s opportunity to recover. [Projectile footsies](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=1510) | Projectile position, direction, speed, separation from its owner. | Whether a particular response clears it safely and whether the owner can punish that response. |
| **Hit confirmation becomes perceptual habit.** Maj describes learning early differences between blocking and hit reactions “almost subconsciously.” Players also use health changes or sound; he cautions against assuming every single attack is practically confirmable. [Hit-confirmation methods](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=4434) | Hit/block reactions, impact effects, health loss, displacement. | Available follow-ups, cancel windows, and whether the target remains in range. Audio cues are unavailable to this model. |
| **Throws demand fine spacing.** A classic SRK discussion emphasizes that very small range differences decide throw situations. [SRK throw discussion](https://archive.supercombo.gg/t/question-about-a-weird-glitch-spd-whiffed-on-a-dizzed-chun/56250) | Near-contact spacing, approach, landing, wake-up. | Exact throw reach and eligibility. Looking close does not establish that either fighter can currently throw. |
| **Corners change movement options.** Maj highlights the basic advantage: a cornered opponent cannot evade by backing away. [Footsies handbook](https://sonichurricane.com/?page_id=1702) | Each fighter’s remaining retreat space; side changes. | How to exploit or escape that position. |
| **Health and time change acceptable risk.** Thelo explicitly includes life advantage and remaining time when deciding whether to wait reactively or take initiative. [Reaction-based defense](https://forums.sirlingames.com/t/thelos-quick-guide-to-reaction-based-defense/1043) | Health lead, remaining health, clock. | Strategy for protecting a lead or recovering a deficit. |

This supports your “eye and muscle memory” concept, with one qualification: **general output labels still require learning varied visual appearances.** Recognizing “hit reaction” across different sprites is general perception; knowing exactly which retaliation beats a particular attack is matchup knowledge.

Conditioning belongs in temporal memory: Maj’s walk-up throw example depends on earlier pokes creating hesitation. Two frames can show hesitation-like stillness, but cannot establish that conditioning caused it. Against CPU opponents, treat conditioning as a hypothesis about repeatable AI responses, not an assumption that human psychology applies. [Chapter 1](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=691)

**Assessment of the existing questions**

| Existing question | Assessment |
|---|---|
| **1. Close / mid / far?** | Keep, but add **point-blank** and **closing / steady / separating**. Broad distance bins miss range-boundary crossings. No universal distance bin equals “safe” or “throwable.” |
| **2. Attacking: yes/no?** | Replace. Startup, active attack, and recovery imply different responses. Also inspect **your own state**: seeing an opening is useless if you are still stunned or recovering. A projectile can remain threatening after its owner stops attacking. |
| **3. Airborne: yes/no?** | Keep and enrich. Rising versus falling, approaching versus retreating, and possible side crossing determine urgency. Apply to both fighters. |
| **4. Move X better than walking in?** | Mis-framed as perception. “Better” requires an objective, time horizon, own-action properties, and assumptions about the opponent’s next action. A learned immediate-outcome estimate is useful, but should be a separate, uncertain action assessment. |
| **5. Health categories?** | Keep; add **health lead and clock**. Four bins can hide a decisive small lead. Preserve numerical fractions internally, even if language output stays coarse. |

**Proposed questions, prioritized**

The following are my design recommendations, informed by the cited player material. RAM derivations are implementation proposals, **not a verified SNES address map**. “Visible” means recoverable from two timestamped images in favorable cases; every uncertain field should support `unknown`.

| # | Improved question and compact answer | Why players care | Visible in two frames? | Automatic training label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **What state is each fighter in?** `neutral; attack startup/active/recovery; hitstun; blockstun; knocked down/getting up; being thrown; dizzy`. Include standing/crouching where applicable. | Separates danger, inability to act, and apparent openings; supports wake-up pressure and recovery punishment. [Thelo](https://forums.sirlingames.com/t/thelos-quick-guide-to-reaction-based-defense/1043), [Maj](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=759) | **Partial.** Broad states often show clearly. Exact active/recovery boundaries and first actionable frame may share identical imagery. | Map action-state/substate and animation progression to generic classes. Use attack-box activation and decoded animation timing to identify phases. Separate hitstop from recovery. Map dizzy and knockdown flags separately. |
| **2** | **What is our spacing and relative movement?** `point-blank/close/mid/far × closing/steady/separating`, plus opponent left/right. | Supports range control, whiff baiting, and awareness of throw proximity. [Maj](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=691), [SRK](https://archive.supercombo.gg/t/question-about-a-weird-glitch-spd-whiffed-on-a-dizzed-chun/56250) | **Mostly.** Exact collision boundaries are invisible. | Compute horizontal gap from positions and body collision boxes; normalize to viewport width. Bin using fixed documented thresholds. Derive gap change over the observed interval. **Do not label point-blank as guaranteed throw range.** |
| **3** | **What is each fighter’s airborne trajectory?** `grounded/rising/apex/falling × approaching/retreating/vertical`; `crossing sides/unclear`. | Anti-air timing depends on approach and landing geometry, not merely airborne status. [Thelo](https://forums.sirlingames.com/t/thelos-quick-guide-to-reaction-based-defense/1043) | **Mostly.** Near apex, during hitstop, or with overlapping sprites, direction can be ambiguous. | Airborne flag, height, vertical velocity or position differences, relative horizontal motion, and changes in player ordering. Keep airborne knockback distinct through question 1. |
| **4** | **Where are incoming projectiles, and how soon could they reach me?** `none/approaching/passing`; position, height, `soon/later/unclear`. | The owner’s animation does not describe the whole threat. Travel gives time to block, move, or prepare another response. [Projectile footsies](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=1510) | **Mostly after appearance.** Two frames estimate motion; before appearance, projectile intent may be unknowable. | Read projectile objects, ownership, coordinates, and velocity. Estimate time to intersect the current player region under explicitly fixed-motion assumptions. Label each object, rather than collapsing several threats into one. |
| **5** | **What contact just occurred?** For each side: `hit/blocked/whiff/no contact/unclear`. | Distinguishes continuing offense, stopping an unsafe follow-up, and exploiting a miss. [Hit-confirmation methods](https://sonichurricane.com/?p=4434) | **Partial.** Contact may occur entirely between samples. Health loss alone cannot reliably distinguish hit from blocked damage. | Log collision outcomes and hit/block state transitions over the sampled interval; associate them with attack instances. Label whiff only once the relevant attack window ends without contact. Mask events lacking visible evidence in either image. |
| **6** | **How much retreat space does each fighter have?** `cornered/near corner/open`; current side. | Retreat-based evasion disappears at the corner; side switches change defensive direction. [Footsies handbook](https://sonichurricane.com/?page_id=1702) | **Usually near a corner.** Screen edge alone is insufficient when the camera scrolls. | Distance from world position/body boundary to stage movement limits; bin with fixed thresholds. Use camera offset to align labels with the rendered scene. |
| **7** | **What are the health fractions, health lead, and remaining time?** Coarse health labels plus `ahead/even/behind` and clock. | Determines whether waiting, trading, or accepting risk serves the round. [Thelo](https://forums.sirlingames.com/t/thelos-quick-guide-to-reaction-based-defense/1043) | **Yes**, subject to HUD resolution and transition effects. | Read health/max-health and timer; derive fractions and lead. Align with displayed bar values if the HUD animates damage. No meter field for this version. |
| **8** | **For candidate action X, what immediate outcome is plausible?** `connects/blocked/whiffs/interrupted/trades/uncertain`, with confidence. | Preserves practiced action–situation associations without claiming strategic superiority over walking. Spatial interactions are central to Sirlin’s SF2 teaching. [SF tutorials](https://www.sirlin.net/articles/street-fighter-tutorial-videos) | **An estimate, not an observable fact.** Requires knowing the candidate’s own behavior. Future opponent choices remain unknown. | From a saved emulator state, execute X across sampled opponent continuations; record contact, damage, interruption, and position over a fixed horizon. Aggregate outcome frequencies. This requires **counterfactual rollouts**, not one RAM snapshot. |

**Training boundaries that matter**

- **RAM truth can exceed visual evidence.** Hidden accumulated stun, throw immunity, input charge, and exact remaining recovery may be available to a labeler without being identifiable from two images. Super Turbo community research documents internal stun accumulation and timers; do not equate those with visible dizziness or copy their numerical rules into SNES labels. [SRK dizzy research](https://archive.supercombo.gg/t/dizzy-thread-and-values/125226)
- **Match RAM to rendered frames.** Existing arcade tooling explicitly handles display delay. Its `ssf2` profile decodes players, projectiles, positions, and collision boxes, demonstrating the labeling approach—but those addresses are **arcade addresses, not SNES addresses**. [SF2 hitbox script](https://github.com/peon2/fbneo-training-mode/blob/master/hitboxes/sf2-hitboxes.lua)
- **Validate the target ROM’s fields.** Memory search and typed address definitions provide a practical starting point; health and coordinates are easier than animation-phase and collision decoding. [Stable-Retro integration documentation](https://stable-retro.farama.org/v0.9.1/integration/)
- **Do not teach certainty from invisible distinctions.** Use confidence, soft targets, or masked supervision where identical image pairs correspond to different internal states. Candidate-action rollouts against one deterministic CPU state describe that CPU continuation, not universal move success.

**Recommendation:** Make questions 1–7 the compact perceptual interface, emphasizing both fighters’ state, relative motion, projectiles, and contact outcomes. Keep question 8 as an optional learned action assessor with explicit uncertainty and a fixed short horizon. Give the execution layer its own input history and give the language model temporal opponent history, matchup details, and strategic choice. This preserves the useful player analogy: practiced recognition triggers prepared responses, while the system remains honest about what two frames cannot reveal.
